Fundamentals of Entrepreneurial Management-2 

(FUNDII)





Introduction

This course is designed to immerse participants in the entrepreneurial journey through a hackathon-based experiential learning format. Participants will explore the early-stage process of entrepreneurship, from identifying emerging trends and defining problems to tackle, to building prototypes, testing assumptions, and pitching their concepts to stakeholders. The integration of AI tools throughout the course enhances creativity, productivity, and decision-making, preparing students to thrive in dynamic, technology-driven environments.



Objectives

By the end of the course, participants will:

Understand and apply entrepreneurial frameworks to identify opportunities and solve problems.

Develop and test Minimum Viable Products (MVPs) based on validated assumptions.

Leverage AI tools to enhance ideation, prototyping, and pitching.

Gain hands-on experience in pivoting, iteration, and feedback integration.

Deliver compelling venture pitches to diverse stakeholders.

Content

The course will cover the following topics:

Entrepreneurship as a Process: Understanding the iterative nature of entrepreneurship and team dynamics.

Trend Analysis & Opportunity Identification: Exploring macro/micro trends and weak signals to frame strategic opportunities.

Problem Discovery: Utilizing frameworks like Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) and friction mapping to define impactful problem statements.

Value Proposition Design: Crafting customer-centric solutions using personas, hypothesis mapping, and value canvases.

Prototyping & Assumption Testing: Building and testing MVPs to validate critical assumptions.

Feedback Integration: Extracting insights from stakeholders to refine ideas and pivot effectively.

Business Model Development: Structuring monetization strategies and understanding key metrics like CLV (Customer Lifetime Value) and CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost).

Pitching & Storytelling: Developing and delivering persuasive venture pitches.

Live Pitch Day: Presenting ideas to a jury and audience for real-time feedback and evaluation.

The focus is on learning through behavior rather than verbal feedback, and identifying opportunities for refinement before testing with real users.



Expected Learning Outcomes:

Identify key behavioral patterns and unexpected insights to refine their solutions

Session 11 & 12: Pitch Day - Final Presentations

Showcase the top venture ideas from each section in a high-stakes, school-wide pitch event. Provide finalists with an opportunity to present in front of a broader jury and audience.



These final sessions bring together the winning teams from each section for a live pitch event in Aula Magna.
